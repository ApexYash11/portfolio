CASE STUDY

STOCKIQ

End-to-end inventory, forecasting, and demand intelligence for D2C businesses. Converting uncertainty into deterministic, explainable business actions.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

StockIQ is a production-ready intelligence system that transforms raw operational data (orders, inventory events, vendors, campaigns) into probabilistic demand forecasts, optimized multi-warehouse inventory decisions, and constraint-aware reorder recommendations.

The system goes beyond traditional demand forecasting—it converts uncertainty into deterministic, explainable business actions: exactly how much to reorder, where to place inventory, and whether to allow Cash-on-Delivery (COD) based on Return-to-Origin (RTO) risk assessment.

CORE CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

01

DEMAND FORECASTING

Moving beyond point estimates to probabilistic forecasts (P10/P50/P90) that capture uncertainty and enable asymmetric reorder decisions.

02

MULTI-WAREHOUSE ALLOCATION

Intelligently splitting demand across warehouses using stable shares and invariants to avoid demand multiplication while respecting risk floors.

03

CONSTRAINT-AWARE REORDERING

Converting forecasts into actionable reorders that respect lead times, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and safety stock policies.

04

COD/RTO INTELLIGENCE

Assessing return-to-origin risk and translating it into explainable, policy-driven COD eligibility decisions that reduce logistics waste.

KEY FEATURES

Probabilistic Demand Forecasting

SARIMAX-based time-series forecasting with quantile outputs (P10/P50/P90) instead of point estimates, enabling uncertainty-aware planning.

SKU Routing & Fallbacks

Intelligent routing that chooses forecasting strategy per SKU and gracefully falls back to simpler baselines when data is sparse.

Multi-Warehouse Optimization

Event-sourced inventory modeling across multiple warehouses with stable allocation shares and risk floor enforcement.

MOQ-Aware Reorder Logic

Converts forecasts into reorder decisions that respect vendor constraints (lead time, MOQ) and service-level policies.

COD & RTO Intelligence

Rule-based, explainable COD risk assessment based on historical RTO rates and delivery stability, with clear policy actions.

Production-Ready Artifacts

API-ready decision tables and audit CSVs suitable for serving via REST APIs or integration with downstream systems.

DECISION FLOW EXAMPLE

For SKU123 across Warehouse W1:

1. Demand Forecasting: SARIMAX generates weekly demand quantiles: P10=80, P50=120, P90=170 units

2. Inventory State: Current stock position is 90 units; vendor lead time is 2 weeks; MOQ is 200

3. Reorder Decision: Plan demand at P90 (higher service level) → lead-time demand ≈ 2 × 170 = 340. MOQ constraint forces recommended order to be at least 200 units.

4. COD Risk Assessment: Historical analysis shows HIGH RTO risk → policy action becomes DISABLE_COD to reduce logistics waste.

5. Final Output: Reorder recommendation: 200 units; COD Policy: DISABLE_COD

WHY THIS IS PRODUCTION-READY

  • ✓ Deterministic Logic: Routing, fallbacks, reorder constraints, and COD policies are explicit and testable.
  • ✓ Explainable Decisions: Quantiles + policy thresholds make the reasoning legible to stakeholders.
  • ✓ Model Serialization: SARIMAX models can be saved/loaded for reproducible inference.
  • ✓ API-Ready Outputs: Decisions are structured as flat records suitable for JSON and downstream systems.
  • ✓ Offline/Online Separation: Forecasting runs offline; serving is a thin layer reading the latest artifacts.
